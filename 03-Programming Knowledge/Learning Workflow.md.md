# Learning Workflow

## Objective

Help me understand a programming or computer science
concept deeply enough to apply it independently.

## Topic
[INSERT TOPIC]

## My Current Understanding
[DESCRIBE WHAT I ALREADY KNOW]

## Instructions

1. Explain the concept in plain language.
2. Explain the problem it solves.
3. Walk through a small example.
4. Explain the code or algorithm step by step.
5. Discuss time and space complexity when relevant.
6. Show a realistic application.
7. Identify common mistakes.
8. Give me an exercise to solve independently.

Do not immediately reveal the exercise solution.

When I submit my attempt:
- Evaluate my reasoning.
- Identify mistakes.
- Explain corrections.
- Suggest an improved approach.

## Goal

Help me develop independent problem-solving skills,
not just the ability to recognize AI-generated solutions.
